Privacy Policy for Nova IPTV

Last updated: 12 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nova IPTV ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Nova IPTV collects the following limited information to operate the app's licensing and trial system:

  • Device Identifier: A unique device ID is generated to verify trial eligibility and license activation, and to prevent misuse of free trials.
  • License Code: If you activate a paid license, the code you enter is stored and linked to your device ID to verify your subscription status.
  • Usage and Analytics Data: We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data such as app interactions, session duration, device model, operating system version, and general location (country/region level only).

We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify trial period and license/subscription status
  • To prevent abuse of free trials (e.g. multiple trial activations on the same device)
  • To provide access to app features based on your license status

3. Data Storage and Third Parties

Device identifiers, license data, and analytics data are stored and processed using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Analytics), third-party cloud services provided by Google. Firebase's own privacy practices apply to data processed on their infrastructure. We do not sell or share your data with any other third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Payments

If you purchase a subscription or lifetime license, payment is processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment card details — this is handled directly by Google.

5. Data Retention

Device and license data is retained for as long as necessary to provide the licensing service. You may request deletion of your device data by contacting us using the details below.

6. Your Rights

You may request that we delete the data associated with your device ID at any time by contacting us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

Nova IPTV is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
